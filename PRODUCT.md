# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers at UK accounting firms assessing Abdul Muntakim for a trainee accountant or ACCA-track role. They usually arrive from LinkedIn, a job application or a name search, scan quickly, and decide whether he is worth contacting.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that presents Abdul Muntakim, an early-career accounting professional in London, as a credible, careful and personable candidate for professional accountancy. Success means the visitor connects or gets in touch on LinkedIn, and that his name and accounting topics are easy to find through search.

## Positioning

Pairs hands-on trainee accounting experience (Emulous Accounting) with client-facing and business-development work, summed up as "A head for numbers. A focus on people." The insights articles show he can explain UK tax topics clearly, which a CV alone cannot show.

## Operating Context

- Visitors come from LinkedIn, job applications and Google searches for his name or tax topics.
- The CV (`cv.html`) is read on screen and saved or printed to PDF from the browser.
- The insights articles (`insights/`) are educational pieces based on current UK guidance, with source links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: portfolio (`index.html`), online CV with print layout (`cv.html`), insights articles on IR35, Corporation Tax and Making Tax Digital for Income Tax.
- Static site published on GitHub Pages; animation source in `src/effects.js` is built to `effects.js` with `npm run build`.
- Search visibility matters: keep structured data (schema.org Person/WebSite), page titles, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` accurate.
- Primary call to action is LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/abdul-muntakim-84625932b

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Abdul Muntakim. Tagline in use: "A head for numbers. A focus on people."
- Voice: plain, modest and factual, written in the first person, fitting an early-career professional.

## Evidence on Hand

- Experience: Trainee Accountant at Emulous Accounting (London); Event Steward at Wembley Stadium.
- Education: The Compton School, A-Level Accounting (C), BTEC Business (DD), GCSEs as listed on the site.
- Programmes (2025): KPMG Virtual Work Experience, Forvis Mazars workshop, RSM Accounting Insight; also attended a PwC degree-programme event and a Get Into Accounting event.
- Goal: progress through ACCA to become a Chartered Certified Accountant.
- Absent and must not be fabricated: testimonials, references, client names, results, figures, awards, qualifications or employer endorsements beyond what is listed above.

## Product Principles

1. Never overstate: every claim is one he has made; no invented testimonials, clients or achievements.
2. Credibility first: a recruiter should quickly see experience, skills and direction.
3. Make connecting easy: LinkedIn is the next step everywhere it fits.
4. Be findable: content and metadata support search for his name and topics.
5. Show, don't claim: the insights articles demonstrate clear thinking about UK tax.

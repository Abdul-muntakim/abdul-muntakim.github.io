Making Tax Digital for Income Tax: the staged thresholds

The key qualifying-income thresholds for 2026, 2027 and 2028.

Updated 2 September 2026 • UK

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is being introduced in stages. The date a person is required to start depends on their qualifying income in the relevant earlier tax year.

The staged thresholds

Current GOV.UK guidance sets out the following timetable:

  • Qualifying income over £50,000 for 2024–25: start from 6 April 2026.
  • Qualifying income over £30,000 for 2025–26: start from 6 April 2027.
  • Qualifying income over £20,000 for 2026–27: start from 6 April 2028.
Who this is especially relevant to: sole traders and landlords within Self Assessment can be affected when their qualifying income exceeds the relevant threshold, subject to the detailed rules and exemptions.

What changes in practice?

People brought into Making Tax Digital for Income Tax need to use compatible software and maintain digital records in line with the regime. The change is therefore not simply another year-end deadline; it affects how records are maintained throughout the year.

Why preparation matters

Moving from spreadsheets, paper records or once-a-year bookkeeping to a digital process can take time. Landlords and sole traders approaching a threshold should understand their position early enough to choose suitable software and improve record keeping before their start date.
